# KDCN Digital Platform

A modern digital platform developed by KINGMAN DIGITAL CYBER NETWORK (KDCN).
A modular digital platform designed to bring together KDCN's public website, client services, commerce capabilities, business operations, and digital intelligence within an expandable architecture. Long-term vision: an integrated KDCN digital ecosystem.

## 🌍 Live Website

https://kingman37-net.github.io/kdcn-digital-platform/

---

## Mission

To provide secure, modern, AI-powered digital services for individuals, businesses, and organizations.

Repository Structure

Everything published to the live site lives inside docs/.
Source-only files (README, ROADMAP, CHANGELOG) stay at the repository root.

```
KDCN-Digital-Platform/
├── README.md              ← source
├── ROADMAP.md             ← source
├── CHANGELOG.md           ← source
├── .gitignore
│
└── docs/                  ← published site (GitHub Pages)
    ├── index.html
    ├── about.html
    ├── services.html
    ├── portfolio.html
    ├── contact.html
    ├── faq.html
    ├── privacy.html
    ├── terms.html
    ├── careers.html
    ├── blog.html
    ├── linktree.html
    ├── livestream.html
    ├── resources.html
    ├── assets/
    │   ├── css/
    │   └── js/
    │
    ├── client/            ← client portal
    ├── commerce/          ← commerce layer
    ├── business/          ← business admin
    ├── intelligence/      ← AI features
    ├── blog/              ← blog posts
    └── resources/         ← downloadable resources
```
